Write an operating checklist for a fictional neighbourhood repair cafe. The cafe has eight volunteers, three work tables, and two evening sessions each week. Visitors bring small household objects with a short description of the fault. Plan the intake record, label uncertain diagnoses, and explain how to keep parts with their objects. Describe a queue that treats visitors fairly, a simple handover between volunteers, and a closing routine. Include checks for missing labels, duplicate records, and objects waiting for parts. Use plain language, short headings, and actions that a new volunteer can follow. Do not invent statistics or cite organisations. Finish with a short procedure for reviewing the records each month.
