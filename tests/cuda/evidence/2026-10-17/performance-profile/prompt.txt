Draft a practical checklist for a fictional community seed library. There are twelve volunteers, four labelled storage cupboards, and three weekly opening sessions. Packets arrive from local gardeners with a plant name, collection date, and approximate seed count. The library needs a simple intake record, a way to mark uncertain labels, a dry storage routine, and a fair lending procedure that a new volunteer can follow. Explain the steps in their working order. Include checks for duplicate records, damaged packets, and returns. Use plain language, short headings, and specific actions. Do not invent statistics or cite organisations. Finish by describing how the volunteers can review the records each month.
