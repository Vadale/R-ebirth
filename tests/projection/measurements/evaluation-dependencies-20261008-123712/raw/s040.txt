 Five squared is 25.
To solve this problem, we need to understand the concept of squaring a number. Squaring a number means multiplying the number by itself. In mathematical terms, this is represented as n^2, where n is the number we are squaring.
Let's break down the process of squaring a number:
1. Identify the number we want to square.
2. Multiply the number by itself.
3. The result of this multiplication is the square of the number.
Let's apply this process to the problem of finding the square of five:
1. Identify the number we want to square: In this case, the number we want to square is 5.
2. Multiply the number by itself: 5 * 5 = 25.
3. The result of this multiplication is the square of the number 5.
Therefore, five squared is 25.
To further illustrate this concept, let's consider a few examples:
- 3 squared: 3 * 3 = 9
- 4 squared: 4 * 4 = 16
- 5 squared: 5 * 5 = 25
- 6 squared: 6 * 6 = 36
