 Two days is equal to 24 hours. 

To explain this in detail:

1. Understanding the concept of hours: 
   - A day is a unit of time that is equal to 24 hours.
   - This is a fundamental unit of time in the International System of Units (SI).

2. Converting days to hours:
   - To convert days to hours, we need to multiply the number of days by the number of hours in a day.
   - The number of hours in a day is 24.

3. Performing the calculation:
   - Two days is equal to 24 hours.

4. Verification:
   - To verify this, we can use the formula:
     \[
     \text{Number of hours} = \text{Number of days} \times \text{Number of hours in a day}
     \]
   - Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Number of hours} = 2 \times 24 = 48
     \]
   - This confirms that two days is indeed equal to 48 hours.

5. Conclusion:
   - Therefore, the answer to the question "How many hours are in two days? " is 24 hours
