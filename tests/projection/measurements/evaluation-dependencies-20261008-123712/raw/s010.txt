 The capital of Norway is Oslo. 

Explanation: Oslo is the largest city in Norway and serves as the capital of the country. It is located in the northwestern part of the country, near the Arctic Circle. The city is known for its rich history, culture, and natural beauty, including its fjords and mountains. Oslo is also home to the Norwegian Parliament building, the Royal Palace, and the Norwegian Parliament House. The city is also known for its coffee culture, with many coffee shops and cafes in the area. As the capital, Oslo plays a crucial role in the country's governance, economy, and social life.
