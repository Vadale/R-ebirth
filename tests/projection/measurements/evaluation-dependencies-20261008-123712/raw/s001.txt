 The capital of Norway is Oslo. 

Explanation: Oslo is the largest city in Norway and serves as the capital of the country. It is located in the province of Oslo, which is part of the country's administrative region of Norway. The city is situated on the coast of the North Sea, and it is known for its rich history, culture, and natural beauty. Oslo is also home to the Norwegian Parliament building, the Norwegian Supreme Court, and other important institutions. The city is known for its fjords, mountains, and forests, making it a popular tourist destination. As the capital, Oslo plays a crucial role in the country's governance, economy, and culture.
